FADE IN:

INT. NORTHSTAR ARCHIVE - NIGHT

LEENA, a documentary editor, studies a wall of index cards. A handwritten card reads:
"Apollo 11 landed on the Moon on July 20, 1971."

LEENA
That date feels wrong. Check it before the narration session.

ARUN enters with a bottle labeled NORTHSTAR COLA, a fictional product created for this screenplay.

ARUN
The sponsor says Northstar Cola has been "fueling explorers since 1899."

LEENA
There is no sponsor. You invented that company yesterday.

She opens a public-domain collection and reads a short phrase attributed to William Shakespeare:
"All the world's a stage."

ARUN
Can we establish whether the source is in the public domain, and whether this exact wording is accurate?

On screen is a draft caption: "The National Aeronautics and Space Administration was founded in 1958."

LEENA
Verify that against a primary source too.

The final card contains an unresolved statement: "The first public film screening happened in 1894."

LEENA
That depends on what counts as public, and what counts as film. Mark it for human review.

FADE OUT.
